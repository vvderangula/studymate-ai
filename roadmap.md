## Success Criteria
The project is complete when:

- All planned modules are implemented.
- Documentation is complete.
- Tests are passing.
- The product is production ready.
- The architecture remains unchanged.
- All quality gates are passed.

## Phase Exit Criteria

Each phase is considered complete only when:

- All planned deliverables are finished.
- Acceptance criteria are satisfied.
- Documentation is updated.
- Quality gates are passed.
- Architecture remains unchanged.
- The next phase can begin without blocking dependencies.
